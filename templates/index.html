04 — METHODOLOGY & LIMITATIONS

Transparent by design.

Analysis is exploratory and descriptive, built with Tableau Public. The training dataset contains synthetic data. Summed sales fields, manufacturer records, and patterns should not be interpreted as real market share or real-world sales estimates. Missing values exist in source columns, so each view's available records may differ.

Technology: Flask, HTML, CSS, Tableau Public, Visual Studio Code.
